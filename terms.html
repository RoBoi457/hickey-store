STORE TERMS

Store terms

Draft updated 27 October 2026. These draft terms describe the basic rules for using the Hickey storefront and placing an order. Hickey owner review is required before publication.

Accounts

You need a Hickey account to add products to a basket. Keep your sign-in details secure and use accurate contact and delivery information. Your saved basket and new orders are associated with your account.

Products, prices and orders

Product descriptions, availability and prices are shown on the storefront and again before payment. The checkout price is the amount submitted for payment. An order is treated as paid only after the payment provider confirms it. If an item or payment cannot be fulfilled, Hickey will contact you using the details on the order.

Payment

Online payments are handled through Razorpay Checkout. Payment credentials are entered with the payment provider. Hickey records order and payment status and references to support order fulfillment and customer service.

Delivery

Hickey currently estimates delivery may take up to two weeks. This is an estimate, and timing can vary with the delivery location, production and carrier. Please provide a complete address and a phone number that can be used for delivery.

Cancellations, returns and damaged items — owner review required

Hickey must confirm its cancellation, return, refund, replacement and damaged-item policy before this draft is published. Customers can contact support.hickey@gmail.com with an order reference for help. Applicable consumer rights are not limited by this draft.

Contact

Questions about an order or these draft terms: support.hickey@gmail.com.
