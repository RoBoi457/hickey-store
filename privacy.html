PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Draft updated 27 October 2026. This notice describes the information the Hickey storefront uses to provide customer accounts, baskets, orders and support.

Information we use

  • Account details: your email address and, if you choose a password, a salted password hash. We do not store your plain-text password. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your verified email address and Google account identifier to create or sign in to your Hickey account.
  • Basket and order details: items and quantities in your saved basket, order contents, order and payment status, and order references.
  • Delivery details: the name, phone number and address you enter at checkout.
  • Support messages: suggestion, feedback and custom-design forms prepare an email in your email app. Nothing is sent to Hickey until you choose to send it.

How we use information

We use these details to sign you in, keep your basket and order history with your account, take and fulfill orders, verify payment status, answer messages you send, protect the storefront from misuse, and meet applicable record-keeping duties.

Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google shares a verified email address and account identifier with Hickey so we can authenticate you and associate your Hickey account, basket and orders. Hickey does not receive your Google password or access to your Gmail, contacts, files or other Google services. We do not store the Google sign-in token after verification or use Google account data for advertising.

Storage and service providers

Account, basket, order and delivery records are stored in Cloudflare D1 for the Hickey storefront. The site uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in; the session expires after 30 days. Essential browser storage may also be used to keep the basket interface in sync.

Google provides the sign-in service. Razorpay processes checkout payments; Hickey receives order and payment references and status, while payment credentials are handled by Razorpay. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

Access, retention and requests

Registered customers can see their email and order history in their account. Hickey administrators can see customer emails, orders and the delivery details needed to fulfill them. We keep account and order information while it is needed to provide the service, support orders, prevent fraud and meet applicable record-keeping requirements.

To ask about, correct or request deletion of your account information, email support.hickey@gmail.com from the address on your account. Some order or payment records may need to be retained where required for accounting, payment disputes or law.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact support.hickey@gmail.com. Hickey may update this notice when its data practices change and will post the current version on this page.
